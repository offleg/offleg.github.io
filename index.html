About OffLeg

OffLeg LLP builds software without the legacy baggage. Each app starts with one question: what is the shortest path between you and the thing you came to do?

The result is apps that stay out of your way — a word game you can start without signing up, a food diary you can fill in ten seconds after a meal, and a gym log that works like a notebook.
