Word game

WordRun!

The real-time multiplayer word game. Race friends live to build words from scrambled letters — 2 to 4 players, 90-second rounds, no sign-up required.

FreeiOS & Android2–4 playersNo sign-up

What it is

WordRun drops 2 to 4 players into the same round at the same time. Eight, nine or ten letters appear in a circle — swipe across them to connect letters and spell real words before the clock runs out or someone else claims the same word first. No boards, no turns, just everyone racing the same letters live.

How it works

1

Make a room

Create a room and share the code, or join a friend's with theirs. No account or sign-up needed to play.

2

Swipe words

Swipe across the letter circle to form 3 to 10 letter words within each 90-second round.

3

Score big

Longer words and using every letter on the board score more — but the same word claimed twice pays less the second time.

4

Win the game

Play three rounds per game and see who comes out on top of the scorecard.

Questions

Anything else? Email contact@offleg.com.

Is WordRun free?

Yes, WordRun is free to download and play on both iOS and Android.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Pick a display name and you're in — there's no sign-up or login to start a game.

Can iPhone and Android players play together?

Yes. Rooms run on the same live backend regardless of platform, so an iOS player and an Android player can join the same room and play in real time.

How many players does a game need?

Each room supports 2 to 4 players. Create a room, share the code with your friends, and start whenever everyone's in.

What happens if I lose connection mid-round?

Rooms are live over a real-time connection, so reconnecting takes you back into the round in progress.
